Hardware Evolution

From vacuum tubes to integrated circuits, transistors to microprocessors — each breakthrough enabled exponential growth in computing power.

Software Revolution

Programming languages evolved from machine code to high-level languages, making computers accessible to everyone.

Global Impact

Computing transformed society — from business to medicine, education to entertainment, reshaping our world.
